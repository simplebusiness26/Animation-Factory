# Episode 001 — Voice Notes

## Narrator
Warm, playful storyteller. Clear British or neutral English delivery. Slightly dramatic when describing tiny problems, as if narrating an epic rescue. Never sarcastic toward the child characters.

## Captain Pip
Small voice with maximum seriousness. Crisp, confident, urgent, slightly theatrical. Short sentences. Treats everything as official rescue protocol.

Sample direction: "Tiny commander giving the most important briefing in the galaxy."

## Bloop
Bright, enthusiastic, innocent, expressive. Slightly higher pitch than the others. Delivers lines with genuine excitement, never snark.

Sample direction: "Curious kid-energy alien who thinks everything on Earth is amazing."

## Zig
Quick, clever and overconfident. Talks a little faster than the others, especially when explaining inventions. Should sound excited by his own ideas.

Sample direction: "Young inventor certain that every gadget will work perfectly."

## Momo
Deep, soft, gentle and slow. Very few words. Warm and reassuring rather than booming.

Sample direction: "Huge cuddly creature speaking carefully and kindly."

## Human child
Natural, calm, friendly, slightly amused by the aliens. Should sound like a real child character, not exaggerated or babyish.

## Recording strategy for the free pilot
For the first production pass, prioritize narrator + a small number of short character lines. Do not require perfect lip-sync on every shot. Use reaction sounds, pauses and off-camera lines where helpful. This keeps the first episode achievable while preserving quality.
