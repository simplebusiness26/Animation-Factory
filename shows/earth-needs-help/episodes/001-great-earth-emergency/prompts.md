# Episode 001 — Image + Motion Prompts

These prompts assume the locked Earth Needs Help character references are supplied with generation.

## Global visual lock
Original polished 3D children's comedy animation. Bright, warm, tactile materials, soft rounded forms, expressive readable faces, gentle cinematic lighting, strong silhouettes, safe playful environments. Preserve exact character designs, colours, clothing, body proportions and recurring locations. 16:9 landscape. No text, no logos, no extra characters unless requested.

## Shot 01 — Alarm!
### Still prompt
Inside a tiny colourful alien rescue spaceship bridge travelling peacefully through deep space. Captain Pip is at the controls, Bloop sits nearby, Zig holds a small gadget, and huge fluffy Momo sits calmly behind them with a snack. Red emergency lights have just switched on. The crew is caught at the first instant of surprise. Wide cinematic composition, clear view of all four characters, polished children's 3D comedy animation, preserve exact locked character references.

### Motion prompt
A sudden emergency alarm activates. Red warning lights begin flashing softly. Captain Pip snaps upright and urgently leans toward the control panel. Bloop gasps and bounces slightly in surprise. Zig accidentally drops his small gadget and reacts. Momo stays calm, blinking and continuing to chew. Gentle cinematic camera push forward. Natural expressive movement, no redesigns, no extra characters, preserve faces and proportions exactly.

## Shot 02 — Earth needs us
### Still prompt
Same spaceship bridge and exact same characters. The large front window now reveals beautiful blue Earth filling the view. Captain Pip stands tiny but heroic, pointing dramatically toward Earth. Bloop presses his round face excitedly against the glass. Zig opens his gadget belt. Momo gives Earth a friendly wave. Bright space backdrop, warm interior lighting, comedic heroic framing.

### Motion prompt
Earth slowly grows larger through the window. Captain Pip points dramatically and gives a serious command. Bloop presses against the glass and wiggles with excitement. Zig quickly checks his tools. Momo waves gently at Earth. Subtle ship vibration and restrained camera movement. Preserve the exact spaceship and character designs.

## Shot 03 — Heroic arrival... almost
### Still prompt
Sunny colourful suburban park on Earth. The tiny alien spaceship has just bounced through a soft green hedge and is stopping crooked in a flowerbed, completely intact. A few leaves float through the air. The hatch is beginning to open. Funny, harmless, energetic landing, not dangerous. Bright blue sky, family-friendly park, polished 3D animation.

### Motion prompt
The tiny spaceship swoops down, gently bounces through the hedge with a soft comedic boing, slides a short distance and stops crooked in the flowerbed. A harmless puff of dust and leaves rises. The hatch pops open and Bloop appears upside down for a beat. Funny physical comedy, smooth motion, no damage or danger.

## Shot 04 — First contact
### Still prompt
In the same sunny park beside the landed spaceship, the human child stands facing the four alien rescuers. Captain Pip marches forward with an extremely serious expression. Bloop peeks curiously from beside Momo. Zig holds a scanner. Momo stands warmly behind them. The child is surprised but curious and unafraid. Medium group composition.

### Motion prompt
Captain Pip takes two determined little steps toward the child and urgently gestures for directions. The child blinks, then slowly points toward a nearby tree. Bloop leans out to see where the child points. Zig raises a scanner. Momo smiles reassuringly. Natural character acting, stable camera, preserve all designs.

## Shot 05 — The crisis revealed
### Still prompt
A colourful child's kite is harmlessly caught in the branches of a leafy park tree. In the foreground, Captain Pip, Bloop, Zig, Momo and the child look upward. The aliens react as if they are witnessing an enormous planetary disaster while the child looks mildly confused. Bright safe park environment, strong comedic contrast.

### Motion prompt
Camera tilts from the aliens up to the harmless kite, then settles back on their reaction. Captain Pip slowly raises an emergency scanner. Bloop's mouth drops open. Zig reaches for several gadgets. Momo looks puzzled. The child gives a small confused shrug. Keep motion controlled and faces consistent.

## Shot 06A — Zig deploys science
### Still prompt
At the base of the kite tree, Zig proudly kneels beside a compact colourful alien rescue gadget. Mechanical arms are folded inside it. Captain Pip watches seriously, Bloop leans in with excitement, the child watches cautiously, and Momo stands behind them. Clean readable composition.

### Motion prompt
Zig proudly presses a button. The rescue gadget unfolds several small mechanical arms and begins humming. Zig strikes a confident inventor pose. Bloop bounces with excitement. Captain Pip nods seriously. The child takes one small step back. Smooth harmless comedy, no violent motion.

## Shot 06B — Advanced rescue chaos
### Still prompt
The same gadget now spins too enthusiastically beneath the tree, harmlessly blowing colourful leaves through the air. Bloop chases after it, Zig looks shocked that his invention has gone off-plan, Captain Pip points and gives orders, the child watches in disbelief, and Momo remains calm. Wide energetic comedy shot.

### Motion prompt
The gadget spins in a small circle, extends its arms in the wrong directions and blows leaves everywhere without hurting anyone. Bloop runs after it. Zig tries to catch it. Captain Pip points urgently. The child watches with an amused, confused expression. Momo barely moves. Fast but readable physical comedy, preserve anatomy and character identity.

## Shot 07 — Momo solves it
### Still prompt
Everything is suddenly calm beneath the tree. Momo stands closest to the branch with the kite. The others have frozen after the failed rescue gadget. Momo simply reaches one enormous fluffy lavender arm toward the kite. The child waits beside him. Warm quiet comedic composition.

### Motion prompt
Hold a brief quiet beat. Momo looks at the kite, looks at everyone else, then gently reaches up, lifts the kite free from the branch and hands it to the child. The child smiles gratefully. The rest of the crew stays frozen in stunned silence. Captain Pip slowly lowers his scanner. Very gentle camera move, warm wholesome timing.

## Shot 08 — Mission accomplished
### Still prompt
The child happily holds the rescued kite in the sunny park while the four aliens celebrate as though they have saved an entire planet. Captain Pip stands in a heroic pose, Bloop jumps with joy, Zig proudly fixes his goggles, and Momo claps gently. Bright uplifting wide shot.

### Motion prompt
The child starts flying the kite. Bloop hops excitedly. Zig proudly adjusts his goggles. Momo claps slowly. Captain Pip plants his hands on his hips in a tiny heroic pose. Warm celebratory movement, gentle camera pull back, preserve all character designs.

## Shot 09 — New emergency
### Still prompt
In the park after the celebration, Bloop holds an alien snack near his side while a cheeky ordinary pigeon stands just behind him, about to steal it. Captain Pip and the others are nearby. The child is holding the kite. Set up a clean visual comedy beat with everyone readable.

### Motion prompt
The pigeon quickly snatches Bloop's snack and takes two small hops away. Bloop freezes, slowly looks down at his empty hand, then turns toward the pigeon in disbelief. The whole alien crew turns. Captain Pip immediately raises the emergency scanner as if a major crisis has begun. Quick comedic push-in at the end. No aggressive chase, no frightening movement.
