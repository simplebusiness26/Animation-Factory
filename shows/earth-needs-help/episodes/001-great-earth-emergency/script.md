# Episode 001 — The Great Earth Emergency

**Target runtime:** 75–90 seconds  
**Format:** comedy-adventure mini-pilot  
**Purpose:** introduce the crew, their misunderstanding of Earth, and the recurring show formula.

## Logline
The alien rescue crew races across space after detecting an emergency on Earth, only to discover the crisis is a child's kite stuck in a tree. Their complicated rescue plan causes comic chaos until Momo quietly solves the problem in one reach.

## Script

### Shot 1 — Alarm! (0:00–0:07)
Inside the tiny alien spaceship, everything is calm. Suddenly the emergency alarm flashes.

**NARRATOR:** Far, far away, the bravest rescue crew in the galaxy received a terrible warning.

Captain Pip snaps upright. Bloop gasps. Zig drops a gadget. Momo keeps calmly chewing a snack.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** Emergency!

### Shot 2 — Earth needs help (0:07–0:14)
Earth fills the front window. Pip points dramatically.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** Earth needs us!

Bloop presses his face to the glass. Zig opens his gadget belt. Momo waves at the planet.

**BLOOP:** Helloooo, Earth!

### Shot 3 — Heroic arrival... almost (0:14–0:22)
The little ship dives through clouds and attempts a grand landing. It gently bounces through a hedge and stops crooked in a flowerbed.

**SFX:** whoosh — boing — poof

The hatch opens. Bloop pops out upside down.

**BLOOP:** Nailed it.

### Shot 4 — First contact (0:22–0:31)
The human child stares at the aliens. Pip marches forward with total seriousness.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** Earthling! Take us to the emergency!

The child blinks, then points toward a nearby tree.

**CHILD:** Um... it's over there.

### Shot 5 — The crisis revealed (0:31–0:39)
A colourful kite is stuck harmlessly in a tree.

The aliens stare in horror.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** It's worse than I feared.

**CHILD:** It's just my kite.

Pip raises a scanner.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** Exactly.

### Shot 6 — Zig's rescue machine (0:39–0:52)
Zig proudly activates a tiny gadget. Mechanical arms unfold, spin too fast, blow leaves everywhere and chase the kite around the branch without reaching it.

**ZIG:** Stand back. Science is happening!

Bloop chases the gadget. Pip shouts instructions. The child watches, confused.

**NARRATOR:** The rescue became very... advanced.

### Shot 7 — Momo's solution (0:52–1:02)
Everything stops. Momo looks at the kite, then at the others. He simply reaches up, gently takes the kite from the branch and hands it to the child.

Silence.

**CHILD:** Thanks, Momo.

Momo smiles.

**MOMO:** Momo help.

Pip slowly lowers the scanner.

### Shot 8 — Mission accomplished (1:02–1:12)
The crew celebrates like they saved the whole planet.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** Earth is safe!

The child smiles and flies the kite.

### Shot 9 — New emergency (1:12–1:20)
Behind Bloop, a pigeon steals his alien snack.

Bloop freezes.

**BLOOP:** ...my snack.

The whole crew turns toward the pigeon.

Pip's scanner flashes.

**CAPTAIN PIP:** NEW EMERGENCY!

Smash to end.
